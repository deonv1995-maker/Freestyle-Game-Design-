## App details

**App name**  
Energy Relay

**Short description**  
Guide a zero-gravity energy orb through lasers, drones and moving platforms.

**Full description**  
Take control of a living energy orb and climb an endless zero-gravity corridor.

Drag to launch, touch again in flight to slow time, then redirect your momentum before hazards close in. Rebound from walls and moving platforms, read laser timing, ram hostile drones, and reach checkpoints to push your distance record higher.

Energy Relay is built around direct one-finger control and short, replayable runs. Every attempt asks you to balance speed with precision as the corridor becomes more dangerous.

Features:
- Direct drag-to-launch controls
- Slow-motion mid-air redirection
- Zero-gravity momentum and rebound physics
- Moving platforms and timed laser hazards
- Drone encounters with visible attack warnings
- Checkpoints, seven lives, and distance progression
- Mobile-first full-screen play

No account is required for the current release.

## First-publication testing note

If the Play Console developer account is a **personal account created after 13 November 2023**, Google currently requires a closed test with at least 12 opted-in testers continuously for at least 14 days before applying for production access. This is an account-level requirement, so confirm it in the account's Play Console dashboard rather than assuming it applies.
