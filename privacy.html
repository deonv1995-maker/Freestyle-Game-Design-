Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Energy Relay is developed by Freestyle Game Design. This policy explains the data practices of the current Google Play release.

Data collection

The current version of Energy Relay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or sensitive user data. The game does not use user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cloud saves, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or external tracking services.

Gameplay data

Gameplay state is processed locally on the device. The current release does not upload scores, progress, identifiers, diagnostics, or gameplay history to a server operated by Freestyle Game Design.

Network access

The Android release packages the game locally inside the application and does not request the Android internet permission for gameplay.

Children and families

Energy Relay does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The Google Play target-audience declaration should match the audience selected for the store release.

Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces accounts, analytics, advertising, online services, or any other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Privacy and support enquiries can be submitted using the developer contact details shown on Energy Relay's Google Play listing.
